Why this box works

The 80 x 50 zones sit in Suite 650, where the building steps out to 191 feet of depth. That extra depth is what makes two full size technical zones possible side by side without cutting into the support spaces.

Suite 600 carries everything that needs walls: the party room on the north end where parents can see the entry, two private offices, the athlete development and weight area, and the two 30 x 40 grids.

Restrooms land on the north end wall of Suite 650, close to the fields and on the same run as the incoming water and sanitary. Four ADA fixtures are shown pending a plumbing fixture count against occupancy.

Overhead doors on the south wall open directly onto the futsal court, so the indoor and outdoor training areas operate as one facility rather than two.

Clear height, column locations and the fixture count all need confirmation against the permit set before this goes to lease exhibit.
