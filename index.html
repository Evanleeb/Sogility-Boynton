Why this box works

Suite 650 is a clear span end bay. It runs 69'6" wide by 192'6" deep with no interior columns. The structural grid at 37'6" and 75'0" lands on the demising wall between 650 and 600, which puts the only two columns in the combined premises inside the circulation aisle between the technical zones and the training grids. Neither 80 x 50 field is obstructed.

That depth is what makes the plan work. The end bays of Building A run about 57 feet deeper than the middle bays, so two full 80 x 50 zones stack end to end with room left over for the restroom block on the north wall.

Suite 600 carries everything that needs walls. The party room sits on the north end where parents see the entry, the two private offices sit behind it, the athlete development floor holds the middle, and the two 40 x 30 grids run south.

Overhead doors on the south wall open onto the futsal court, so the indoor and outdoor training areas run as one facility rather than two.

Shell dimensions, the suite split, the structural grid and the restroom block were taken off Arcadis leasing plan AS1.01 dated 3/7/2024. Clear height and the plumbing fixture count still need confirmation against the permit set.
